About this map

Topsoil (0–20 cm) soil organic carbon (SOC) stock for the Omo-Gibe Basin, Ethiopia, following the uncertainty-aware Quantile Regression Forest approach of Zhao et al. (2026, Geoderma) for Florida grazing lands. Left: median prediction (Q50). Right: 90% prediction-interval width (Q95 − Q05), i.e. how uncertain the estimate is.

Status: the model is trained on synthetic demo soil points, so the pattern only shows how the product will look. It will be replaced by predictions from EthioSoilGrids and field samples with real Earth-observation covariates.
